FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers are based on recent competitions. Anything that changes for 2027 will be updated here.

What is IRLCPC?

IRLCPC is the Irish Collegiate Programming Competition, hosted by UCC's ACM Student Chapter. It's modelled on the ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC), and our chapter sets the problems.

How does the competition work?

Teams get a set of problems to solve within 4 hours. They range from straightforward to very challenging, and nobody is expected to solve them all. Solutions are marked automatically, and you score points for every test your solution passes.

How many people are on a team?

Up to 3 students per team.

Who can take part?

Students in a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate programme at a third-level institution. The competition isn't aimed at complete beginners, but anyone with a passion for programming is welcome.

Which programming languages can we use?

In recent years: Python 3, Go, C, C++ and Java. You can work on Linux or Windows.

What should we bring?

We'll confirm exactly what to bring closer to the event. If you have a question in the meantime, email us at ucccsacm@gmail.com.

How should we prepare?

Practise with past problem sets, free to download on our past events page. On the day:

  • Read the whole problem set first. Problems aren't in order of difficulty.
  • Stuck on a problem for a while? Move on and come back later.
  • A simple brute-force solution often scores a few points.
  • Don't panic!
How do we register?

Through the online registration form linked from the Register button at the top of this page. Payment is made as part of registration.

When and where is IRLCPC 2027?

On 6 March 2027, in the Western Gateway Building, UCC (to be confirmed). See the schedule for the day's plan.

Can my company sponsor the event?

Yes. Email us at ucccsacm@gmail.com and we'll be in touch about partnering with IRLCPC 2027.

I have another question. Who do I ask?

Email us at ucccsacm@gmail.com.
