Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how UCC's ACM Student Chapter ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website.

Who we are

This website is run by UCC's ACM Student Chapter at University College Cork. You can contact us at ucccsacm@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information through this website. It has no accounts, sign-up or contact forms, analytics or tracking, and it does not use cookies or store anything on your device.

Hosting and third-party services

Like any website, when you visit a page your browser connects to the following services, which receive your IP address and basic browser information as part of delivering the page:

  • our hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs for security and operation;
  • Google Fonts, for the website's font;
  • Cloudflare's cdnjs, for the website's icons.

These providers handle that information under their own privacy policies.

Event registration

Registration for our events, including payment, takes place outside this website: the website only links to a registration form hosted by Google Forms. The details you enter on that form are collected by us and used to organise the event you registered for. You need to provide them to register; without them we can't confirm your place. Payment is also handled outside this website. Google's privacy policy applies to its handling of the form.

Invitations to future events

If you have taken part in one of our events, we may use the contact details from your registration to invite you to future events. This is done by our committee, not through this website. If you would rather not hear from us, email ucccsacm@gmail.com and we will stop contacting you.

How long we keep registration details

We keep registration details only for as long as we need them to run our events and to invite past participants back. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Why we can use your details (legal basis)

  • Event registration: to register you for, and run, the event you signed up to.
  • Invitations to future events: our legitimate interest in inviting past participants to similar events we run. You can object at any time and we will stop.
  • Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in replying to you.

We do not use your details for automated decision-making or profiling.

Who we share your details with

We do not sell or share your details with anyone else. They are held using Google Forms and Gmail, and payment is handled by the payment service used for registration. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards set out in its own privacy policy.

Links to other websites

This website also links to services run by others, such as Google Calendar, the Internet Archive, Instagram, X (Twitter) and LinkedIn. Any information you give those services is handled under their own privacy policies, not this one.

If you email us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not share them or add you to any mailing list.

Your rights

Under data protection law (GDPR) you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. To make a request, email us at ucccsacm@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
